Expert support to enhance your security and resilience!
Our range of specialist encryption and decryption services starts by the sender and receiver getting a copy of a common HIVE map. This is used for the encryption and decryption of data between them.
Managed detection and response- The sender will do the setup of encryption and will tune the map using a set of defined parameters. On the other hand, the receiver will tune according to the sender’s map.
Getting ready with the key– The sender will generate an encryption key from the map, which can be used symmetrically with the receiver for the encryption process. The size of the key will vary depending on the agreed parameters between the sender and receiver.
Resolving your security challenges– The encryption process will take place and cipher text is streamed down the insecure communication channel between the sender and receiver. The receiver finally uses the HIVE map and encryption key to decrypt the cipher text into the original plain text that was sent.
